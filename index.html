Who we are

Welcome to the Gators

Founded in 2025, the Gators are a vibrant and welcoming touch rugby club based in Ganshoren, Brussels. Our name draws inspiration from the Ganshoren marsh — rugged, tenacious, and always ready to play.

Whether you're an experienced rugby player or have never touched a ball in your life, you'll find a place with us. We believe sport is for everyone, and we're here to prove it.

The Game

What is Touch Rugby?

Touch rugby is a fast-paced, non-contact variant of rugby where tackles are replaced by a simple touch. Easy to learn, impossible to put down.

✋

Touch, Don't Tackle

Players touch opponents instead of bringing them to the ground — safe and open to everyone.

6️⃣

Six-Touch Rule

A team retains possession through up to six touches before it changes hands.

↩️

Backward Passes Only

Passes must travel backward or sideways — no forward passes allowed.

⚡

Mixed & Inclusive

Teams of 6 with 3 women and 3 men keep every game balanced and exciting.
