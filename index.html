Who we are

Welcome to the Gators

Founded in 2025, the Gators are a vibrant and welcoming touch rugby club based in Ganshoren, Brussels. Our name draws inspiration from the Ganshoren marsh — rugged, tenacious, and always ready to play.

Whether you're an experienced rugby player or have never touched a ball in your life, you'll find a place with us. We believe sport is for everyone, and we're here to prove it.

The Game

What is Touch Rugby?

Touch rugby is a fast-paced, non-contact variant of rugby where tackles are replaced by touches. Easy to learn, hard to put down.

✋

Touch, Don't Tackle

Instead of bringing opponents to the ground, you simply touch them. Safe, fair, and open to all.

6️⃣

Six-Touch Rule

A team can be touched up to six times before possession changes — just like tackles in traditional rugby.

↩️

Backward Passes Only

Passes must go backward or sideways — no forward passes allowed. Build your attack from behind.

⚡

Fast & Inclusive

Mixed teams of 6 (3 women, 3 men) keep the game balanced, inclusive, and exciting for all.
